# Linen care
## Overview
Linen is a strong natural fibre made from flax; it is cool and breathable but wrinkles easily. Typical properties: breathable, strong, absorbent, wrinkles easily.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Machine wash, gentle or normal cycle. Recommended water temperature: 30–40°C. Detergent: Mild detergent. Linen is strong but wrinkles and stiffens when over-dried, so a moderate wash and damp ironing are recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Air dry or tumble dry low. With a dryer: Tumble dry low and remove while slightly damp. Without a dryer: Air dry; shake out and smooth while damp.
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for linen: High (up to 200°C), ideally while damp or with steam.
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Non-chlorine bleach on whites only.
## Special care and what to avoid
Linen wrinkles easily, so iron while damp; over-drying makes it stiff. Avoid: chlorine bleach, over-drying, long soaking of coloured linen.

# Wool care
## Overview
Wool is a natural animal fibre that is warm and elastic but shrinks and felts when exposed to heat and agitation. Typical properties: warm, elastic, insulating, shrink-prone.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Wool or gentle cycle, or hand wash. Recommended water temperature: Cold, up to 30°C. Detergent: Mild wool-safe detergent; avoid enzyme-based detergents. Wool shrinks and felts with heat and agitation, so a cold, gentle wash and flat drying are recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Dry flat. With a dryer: Do not tumble dry. Without a dryer: Dry flat on a towel and reshape while damp.
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for wool: Low (up to 110°C) with a pressing cloth.
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Do not bleach.
## Special care and what to avoid
Avoid high heat and agitation, which cause felting and shrinkage; never hang heavy wet wool. Avoid: hot water, wringing, tumble drying, harsh detergents.

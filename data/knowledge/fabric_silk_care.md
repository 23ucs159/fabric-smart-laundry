# Silk care
## Overview
Silk is a fine natural protein fibre that is smooth and lustrous but weakens with heat, friction and harsh chemicals. Typical properties: smooth, delicate, lustrous, heat sensitive.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Gentle hand wash or delicate machine cycle in a mesh bag. Recommended water temperature: Cold, up to 30°C. Detergent: Mild pH-neutral detergent made for delicates. Silk fibres weaken with heat and harsh detergents, so a gentle, cold wash is recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Air dry flat or hang in shade. With a dryer: Do not tumble dry. Without a dryer: Air dry flat away from direct sunlight.
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for silk: Low (up to 110°C), on the reverse side, no steam.
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Do not bleach.
## Special care and what to avoid
Avoid wringing, direct sunlight, and perfume or deodorant sprays that can stain. Avoid: chlorine bleach, wringing, hot water, long soaking.

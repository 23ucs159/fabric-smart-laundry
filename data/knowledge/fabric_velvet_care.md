# Velvet care
## Overview
Velvet has a soft raised pile that is easily crushed, and some velvets are dry-clean only. Typical properties: soft pile, luxurious, delicate, crush marks.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Gentle cycle or hand wash; check the label, some velvet is dry-clean only. Recommended water temperature: Cold, up to 30°C. Detergent: Mild detergent. Velvet's pile is easily crushed by heat and pressure, so a gentle cold wash with steam-only finishing is recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Air dry hanging, without wringing. With a dryer: Do not tumble dry. Without a dryer: Air dry hanging.
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for velvet: Do not iron directly; steam from the reverse side.
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Do not bleach.
## Special care and what to avoid
Crushing the pile leaves marks; check the care label because some velvets are dry-clean only. Avoid: wringing, direct ironing, tumble drying.

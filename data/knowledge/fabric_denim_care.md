# Denim care
## Overview
Denim is a thick cotton twill that is dyed with indigo, which can fade and bleed in the wash. Typical properties: thick, durable, twill weave, dye bleeds.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Machine wash inside out, normal cycle. Recommended water temperature: 30°C (cold to 30°C). Detergent: Mild detergent for dark colours. Denim dye fades with heat and friction, so a cool, inside-out wash is recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Air dry or tumble dry low. With a dryer: Tumble dry low. Without a dryer: Air dry inside out.
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for denim: High (up to 200°C), inside out.
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Do not bleach.
## Special care and what to avoid
Wash inside out and separately at first because indigo dye can bleed; wash less often to preserve colour. Avoid: bleach, hot water, washing with light garments.

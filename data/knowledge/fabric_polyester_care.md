# Polyester care
## Overview
Polyester is a durable synthetic fibre that dries quickly and resists wrinkles, but it is sensitive to high heat. Typical properties: durable, quick-drying, wrinkle resistant, heat sensitive.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Machine wash, normal or synthetic cycle. Recommended water temperature: 30–40°C. Detergent: Normal detergent. Polyester is heat sensitive, so a moderate wash with a low-temperature iron is recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Air dry or tumble dry low. With a dryer: Tumble dry low. Without a dryer: Air dry.
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for polyester: Low (up to 110°C).
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Avoid chlorine bleach.
## Special care and what to avoid
High heat can melt or permanently crease the fibres; avoid hot irons and high-heat dryers. Avoid: high heat, hot irons, fabric softener build-up.

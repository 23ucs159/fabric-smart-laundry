# Cotton care
## Overview
Cotton is a natural plant fibre that is breathable, absorbent and durable, and it handles regular washing well. Typical properties: breathable, absorbent, durable, easy to wash.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Machine wash, normal or gentle cycle. Recommended water temperature: 30–40°C. Detergent: Normal liquid or powder detergent; use a colour-safe detergent for dark colours. Cotton generally tolerates moderate washing temperatures, so a 30–40°C wash is recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Air dry or tumble dry low. With a dryer: Tumble dry low. Without a dryer: Air dry (line dry in shade).
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for cotton: Medium (up to 150°C).
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Oxygen bleach on whites only; avoid chlorine bleach on coloured cotton.
## Special care and what to avoid
Avoid excessive heat, which can cause shrinkage; wash dark colours separately. Avoid: very hot water, overloading the drum, high-heat tumble drying.

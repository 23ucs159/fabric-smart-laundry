# Rayon care
## Overview
Rayon (viscose) is a soft, semi-synthetic fibre made from cellulose that drapes well but is weak when wet. Typical properties: soft, drapes well, absorbent, weak when wet.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Gentle machine cycle or hand wash. Recommended water temperature: Cold, up to 30°C. Detergent: Mild detergent. Rayon loses strength when wet and shrinks with heat, so a gentle cold wash is recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Air dry; avoid wringing. With a dryer: Tumble dry low only if the care label allows. Without a dryer: Air dry flat or on a padded hanger.
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for rayon: Low to medium (up to 150°C) on the reverse side.
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Do not bleach.
## Special care and what to avoid
Rayon is weak when wet, so avoid wringing and heat; shrinkage is possible. Avoid: wringing, hot water, high heat, long soaking.

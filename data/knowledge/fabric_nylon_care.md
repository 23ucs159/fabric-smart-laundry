# Nylon care
## Overview
Nylon is a strong, lightweight synthetic fibre that is smooth and elastic but melts at high temperatures. Typical properties: strong, lightweight, smooth, heat sensitive.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Machine wash, gentle or synthetic cycle. Recommended water temperature: 30°C. Detergent: Mild detergent. Nylon is strong but heat sensitive, so a gentle wash at a low temperature is recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Air dry or tumble dry low. With a dryer: Tumble dry low. Without a dryer: Air dry.
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for nylon: Low (up to 110°C), or avoid ironing.
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Do not use chlorine bleach (it causes yellowing).
## Special care and what to avoid
Heat can melt nylon; wash apart from lint-shedding fabrics and use a mesh bag for delicate items. Avoid: chlorine bleach, high heat, hot irons.

# Synthetic/Blended care
## Overview
Blended and synthetic fabrics combine fibres, so care must follow the most sensitive fibre in the mix. Typical properties: mixed fibres, easy care, heat sensitive, varies by blend.
## Washing
Recommended washing: Machine wash, gentle or synthetic cycle. Recommended water temperature: 30°C. Detergent: Mild or normal detergent. Blends should be treated according to their most heat-sensitive fibre, so a gentle 30°C wash is recommended.
## Drying
Recommended drying: Air dry or tumble dry low. With a dryer: Tumble dry low. Without a dryer: Air dry.
## Ironing
Ironing temperature for synthetic/blended: Low (up to 110°C), following the most sensitive fibre.
## Bleaching
Bleaching guidance: Avoid bleach unless the label allows it.
## Special care and what to avoid
Follow the care label and use the setting for the most delicate fibre; high heat may shrink, melt or crease the fabric. Avoid: high heat, hot irons, bleach.
